## Non-negotiables
- Rating scales, atlas structure and voice follow `jujitsu.md` (they/them; no invented statistics; no unverifiable attributions).
- Technique pages follow `techniques/_TEMPLATE.md`; combo notation is defined in `drills/README.md`.
- Ruleset facts need a source or a "verify per promotion" note. Wikipedia is blocked here.
- Do not push to `main` or open a PR unless the user asks in that session.
- Specs live in `docs/` (PRD, DRD); content lives at the root, `techniques/` and `drills/`.
