# The Complete Jiu-Jitsu Program

*96 sessions · 4 sessions a week · 24 weeks.*

## Week 1

### Session 1 — Day one — the tap, the mount, the upa

- **Warm-up (8 min):** shrimp / hip escape · bridge (upa) · technical stand-up
- **Concept (3 min):** The tap — voluntary, early, without ego
- **Safety (7 min):** how to tap (two taps, or verbally) · mat etiquette · hygiene · injury communication before the round · never train sick · ego and losing — you will lose constantly for a year, and that is the tuition
- **Position (15 min):** mount (full) · low mount / grapevine mount — riding, and what each gives the bottom player
- **Escape (15 min):** upa / trap-and-roll · elbow-knee escape — choosing between them by where their weight is
- **Live (12 min):** positional sparring — start mounted, 5×90 s, bottom escapes / top holds

### Session 2 — Side control and the frame

- **Warm-up (8 min):** shrimp · reverse shrimp · bridge (upa)
- **Concept (3 min):** Frames — bone structures, not muscle
- **Position (12 min):** side control · kesa gatame (scarf hold) · reverse kesa / modified kesa
- **Escape (20 min):** frame and shrimp · bridge-and-roll (ghost) · kesa escape
- **Live (17 min):** positional sparring — start under side control, 5×90 s

### Session 3 — The back — the worst place to be

- **Warm-up (8 min):** shrimp · bridge (upa) · sit-through / hip heist
- **Concept (3 min):** Posture — head up, spine straight, arms in
- **Position (12 min):** back control (hooks in) · body triangle
- **Escape (18 min):** back escape — hand fight and slide · back escape — hip-to-hip
- **Submission defense (7 min):** chin down, hands fight — surviving the rear naked choke
- **Live (12 min):** back attack vs back defense, 6×90 s, swap every round

### Session 4 — Knee-on-belly, north–south, and the rest of the bad spots

- **Warm-up (8 min):** shrimp · technical stand-up · forward / backward shoulder roll
- **Concept (3 min):** Breathing — nasal, rhythmic, continuous
- **Position (10 min):** knee-on-belly (KOB) · north–south
- **Escape (22 min):** knee push · frame and shrimp out · heel drag / foot drag · hip heist to half
- **Live (17 min):** escape-or-die rotation — mount, side, back, KOB, north–south, 90 s each

## Week 2

- **Position (12 min):** turtle (bottom) · turtle top / back exposure
- **Escape (20 min):** sit-out · stand up in base · granby roll
- **Live (17 min):** turtle wars — turtled player faces or stands, rider takes the back

### Session 6 — Getting off the floor

- **Warm-up (8 min):** technical stand-up · breakfall (side and back) · sprawl
- **Concept (3 min):** Distance management — free, grip, contact
- **Standing (12 min):** sprawl position · stance and motion
- **Escape (18 min):** underhook to dogfight · coming up from bottom side control
- **Live (19 min):** wrestle-up game — bottom stands or takes them down, top flattens

### Session 7 — Submission defense I — the neck

- **Warm-up (8 min):** shrimp · bridge (upa) · reverse shrimp
- **Concept (3 min):** Timing over strength
- **Submission defense (25 min):** chin down, hands fight (rear naked choke) · posture and stack (triangle choke) · hip out and hand fight (guillotine) · Von Flue counter (guillotine from side control)
- **Live (12 min):** submission escape only — one attack per round, applied at a crawl
- **Study (12 min):** early vs middle vs late defense — early defense is a technique, late defense is a prayer

### Session 8 — Submission defense II — the joints

- **Warm-up (8 min):** shrimp · bridge (upa) · forward / backward shoulder roll
- **Concept (3 min):** Position before submission
- **Submission defense (25 min):** hide the thumb / bicep curl (kimura and americana) · straighten the arm · hitchhiker escape (armbar) · posture, hands to the mat (omoplata)
- **Live (12 min):** submission escape only — armbar, kimura, omoplata
- **Test (12 min):** WEEK 2 TEST — escape mount, side control and back at 50% inside 60 s each
