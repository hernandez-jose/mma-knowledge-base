# Partner Drills (`P-`)

> Rungs 2 and 3 of [the ladder](README.md#the-ladder-every-technique-climbs):
> cooperative reps until the shape is automatic, then progressive resistance
> until it survives a partner who knows what's coming.

**Reading a drill:** every card names what it trains, a rep dose, and which
module it serves. Unless it says otherwise, **do both sides**. If time is short,
cut the drill — not the weak side.

**The resistance dial.** Every `P-` drill has three settings. Say the number out
loud before you start.

| Setting | Partner's job | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Feed (0–20%)** | Give the exact reaction the technique needs, at half speed | The first 50 reps, and every new technique forever |
| **Hold (40–60%)** | Defend with one specific counter, named in advance | Once the shape is automatic |
| **Live start (70%+)** | Defend however they like, from the drill's start position | The last two minutes only — then go to a [game](positional-games.md) |

## P-01 · Pummelling

**Atlas:** [Solo movements → *Pummelling*](../../jujitsu.md#part-xi--solo-movements-the-physical-alphabet) · [Clinch positions → *Underhook · Over-under clinch*](../../jujitsu.md#iic--standing-and-clinch-positions)

**Trains:** inside control in the clinch · **Dose:** 3 × 90 s · **Feeds:** Module 09

Standing, chest to chest. Alternate underhooks — as they swim one in, you swim
the other. Keep your head on the same side as your underhook, hips in contact,
elbows tight so they can't get double-unders.

- **Cue:** *Underhook, head position, hips — in that order.*
- **Hold setting:** one partner tries to lock double underhooks; the other must prevent it.
- **Live start:** first to a bodylock with both hands clasped wins the exchange; reset.

## P-02 · Grip fight isolation

**Atlas:** [Concepts → *Grip fighting*](../../jujitsu.md#part-x--the-concepts-more-important-than-any-move) · [Gap analysis XV.a → *Grip fighting as its own subject*](../../jujitsu.md#xva--things-missing-from-almost-every-syllabus)

**Trains:** the exchange that decides every other exchange · **Dose:** 4 × 60 s standing, 4 × 60 s seated · **Feeds:** Module 11

No takedowns, no submissions, no passing. Only grips. **Gi:** score for
establishing a collar-and-sleeve, a two-on-one, or a cross-collar grip and
holding it 3 seconds. **No-gi:** score for a collar tie, a two-on-one, an
underhook, or wrist control held 3 seconds. Break their grip within 2 seconds or
you concede the point.

- **Cue:** *Nobody sweeps you with grips you never allowed.*
- **Why it exists:** grip fighting isn't a "technique" so it never gets a class slot — and it's the biggest single skill gap between blue and purple belt. Five minutes, every class, forever.

## P-03 · Shot/sprawl alternation

**Atlas:** [Wrestling takedowns → *Double leg*](../../jujitsu.md#iiia--wrestling-takedowns-leg-attacks) · [Takedown defense → *Sprawl*](../../jujitsu.md#part-iv--takedown-defense)

**Trains:** the entry and the answer, in the same round · **Dose:** 6 × 30 s, alternating on the whistle · **Feeds:** Module 09

One partner shoots at 60%, the other sprawls. Whistle every 30 seconds and swap
roles. Shooter scores for a clean lift or a knee tap; defender scores for a
sprawl that ends chest-heavy with an angle behind.

- **Cue:** *Sprawl, then circle.* A sprawl that stops in front of them is half a defense.

## P-04 · Penetration step on a moving partner

**Atlas:** [Solo movements → *Penetration step*](../../jujitsu.md#part-xi--solo-movements-the-physical-alphabet) · [Wrestling takedowns → *Double leg*](../../jujitsu.md#iiia--wrestling-takedowns-leg-attacks)

**Trains:** timing, not mechanics · **Dose:** 10 each side · **Feeds:** Module 09

Partner circles and changes direction; you follow, and shoot only when their
weight lands on the near foot. No finish — the rep ends at the knee touching
the mat between their feet.

- **Cue:** *Shoot on their step, not on your decision.*
- **Fail signal:** you shoot from too far and reach with your hands. Take one more step first.

## P-05 · Mount escape reps

**Atlas:** [Positional escapes → *Upa / trap-and-roll · Elbow-knee escape*](../../jujitsu.md#viia--positional-escapes)

**Trains:** trap-and-roll and elbow-knee · **Dose:** 10 each escape, each side · **Feeds:** Module 02

**Upa:** trap the arm at the wrist and the same-side foot with your foot, bridge
high **over that shoulder**, finish inside their guard. **Elbow-knee:** frame at
the hip, shrimp, insert the knee, recover half guard.

- **Cue:** *Make the choice before you move.* Trap-and-roll wants their weight forward; elbow-knee wants it back. Bridge, feel which way they post, then pick.
- **Hold setting:** top rides but doesn't attack. **Live start:** top may attack; bottom escapes or survives 60 s.
- **Detail page:** [mount escapes](../../techniques/escapes/mount-escapes.md)

## P-06 · Side control escape reps

**Atlas:** [Positional escapes → *Frame and shrimp · Underhook to dogfight*](../../jujitsu.md#viia--positional-escapes)

**Trains:** the three answers from under side control · **Dose:** 8 each, each side · **Feeds:** Module 02

**(a) Frame and shrimp** to knee-in and guard recovery. **(b) Underhook** to
dogfight and come up. **(c) Bridge-and-roll** when they're high and heavy on the
far side.

- **Cue:** *Elbow to knee, knee to elbow.* The gap between them is the whole escape.
- **Fail signal:** you shrimp with your arms extended — frames must be short, bone-on-bone, inside your own chest line.
- **Detail page:** [side control escapes](../../techniques/escapes/side-control-escapes.md)

## P-07 · Back escape reps

**Atlas:** [Positional escapes → *Back escape — hand fight and slide*](../../jujitsu.md#viia--positional-escapes)

**Trains:** the hand fight, then the slide · **Dose:** 10 each side · **Feeds:** Module 02

Chin down, both hands win the choking hand (two-on-one), shoulders walk flat to
the mat on the **non-choking** side, hips slide out, clear the top hook, turn in
and land in their guard or side control.

- **Cue:** *Chin buys time, hands buy freedom.*
- **Hold setting:** partner keeps the seatbelt but doesn't attack. **Live start:** partner hunts the RNC at 70%.
- **Detail page:** [back escapes](../../techniques/escapes/back-escapes.md)

## P-08 · Turtle recovery reps

**Atlas:** [Positional escapes → *Sit-out*](../../jujitsu.md#viia--positional-escapes) · [Takedown defense → *Sit-out*](../../jujitsu.md#part-iv--takedown-defense)

**Trains:** getting out before they attach · **Dose:** 8 of each exit · **Feeds:** Module 10

Three exits from turtle with a partner riding: **sit-out** (post and kick
through to face them), **technical stand-up** (when their weight is light), and
**granby roll** (when they're heavy over your hips and you have an underhook to
follow).

- **Cue:** *Turtle is a bus stop, not a house.*
- **Detail page:** [turtle escapes](../../techniques/escapes/turtle-escapes.md)

## P-09 · Guard retention reps

**Atlas:** [Guards → *guard retention*](../../jujitsu.md#iib--guards-bottom-positions-with-attacks) · [Tier 3 — guard → *Guard retention*](../../jujitsu.md#tier-3--guard-weeks-412)

**Trains:** hips and legs against a passer · **Dose:** 4 × 90 s · **Feeds:** Module 04

Passer moves at 40% and only tries to get past the legs — no pins, no
submissions, and they reset to standing every time they clear. Bottom player may
not grip the legs to stall; retention only: frames, hip escapes, leg pummelling,
knee shield insertion.

- **Cue:** *Face them, always.* Retention is a rotation problem before it's a leg problem.
- **Progression:** passer to 70%, then run [G-02 guard retention king of the hill](positional-games.md#g-02--guard-retention-king-of-the-hill).

## P-10 · Sweep reps

**Atlas:** [Sweeps → *Scissor · Hip bump · Butterfly · Old school*](../../jujitsu.md#part-vi--sweeps-and-reversals)

**Trains:** the four sweeps that pay rent · **Dose:** 8 each, each side · **Feeds:** Module 05

**Scissor** (closed guard, off-balance to the knee-through side), **hip bump**
(when they post back), **butterfly elevator** (hook plus underhook, load and
tip), **old school** (half guard, underhook plus far-ankle grab).

- **Cue:** *Sweeps take base away; they don't push weight around.* Kill the post first, then move them.
- **Hold setting:** partner posts a specific hand and you must remove that post before sweeping.

## P-11 · Pass reps

**Atlas:** [Guard passing → *Knee cut · Toreando · Body lock pass*](../../jujitsu.md#part-v--guard-passing)

**Trains:** the three passes worth owning early · **Dose:** 8 each, each side · **Feeds:** Module 06

**Knee cut** (underhook and crossface, slice the knee across, hip to the mat),
**toreando** (both pant grips or shin control, pin the legs to one side and walk
around), **body lock pass** (clasp low around the hips, chest heavy, walk the
legs down).

- **Cue:** *Pass the hips, not the legs.* Legs move; hips are the position.
- **Progression:** [C-04](chain-drills.md#c-04--knee-cut--back-step--leg-drag) chains these instead of treating each as an island.

## P-12 · Pin transition circuit

**Atlas:** [Transitions & back takes → *Side control → mount → back*](../../jujitsu.md#part-ix--transitions-and-back-takes) · [Top positions → *the pins themselves*](../../jujitsu.md#iia--dominant-top-positions)

**Trains:** riding the positional ladder · **Dose:** 5 laps each direction · **Feeds:** Module 07

Partner lies down and offers 20% resistance. Circuit: side control → knee-on-belly
→ mount → technical mount → back control → north–south → back to side control.
Hold each pin for 3 seconds (the IBJJF standard) before moving.

- **Cue:** *Transition on their movement, not on the clock.* Have them turn into you or away, and take whatever that opens.
- **Live start:** they escape at 50%; you re-pin wherever you land.

## P-13 · Catch-and-release submissions

**Atlas:** [Rolling formats → *Catch-and-release*](../../jujitsu.md#xiva--drilling-and-rolling-formats)

**Trains:** entries at speed, without the injuries · **Dose:** 3 × 2 min · **Feeds:** Module 08

Live-ish rolling from a set position, but the moment a submission is locked in —
before any pressure — the attacker **releases** and the round continues. Nobody
taps, nobody gets hurt, everybody gets three times the entries.

- **Cue:** *Own the entry, skip the finish.* The finish is the easy part; the entry is what you're short of.
- **Rule:** for leg locks this is the *only* setting until brown belt or a coach says otherwise.

## P-14 · The choke map circuit

**Atlas:** [Top positions → *Front headlock*](../../jujitsu.md#iia--dominant-top-positions) · [Chokes → *Guillotine · D’Arce · Anaconda*](../../jujitsu.md#viiia--chokes-and-strangles)

**Trains:** the front headlock as a hub, not a technique · **Dose:** 6 laps · **Feeds:** Modules 08, 10

Snap-down → front headlock. From there, pick one exit per lap: **guillotine**
(they're square and low), **D'Arce** (their far arm is in), **anaconda** (their
near arm is in), **back take** (they stand up), **go-behind** (they retreat).

- **Cue:** *The head is the hub; the arm position picks the choke.*
- **Why it exists:** the front headlock system is wrestling's gift to BJJ and most gyms treat it as optional. It connects takedown defense, three chokes and back takes in one position.
- **Detail pages:** [guillotine](../../techniques/submissions/guillotine.md) · [D'Arce](../../techniques/submissions/darce.md) · [anaconda](../../techniques/submissions/anaconda.md)

## P-15 · Leg entanglement entry and extraction

**Atlas:** [Submission defense → *Hide the heel / “boot”*](../../jujitsu.md#viib--submission-defense) · [Leg locks → *Straight ankle lock*](../../jujitsu.md#viiic--leg-locks)

**Trains:** leg lock **defense** before offense · **Dose:** 10 extractions, then 10 entries · **Feeds:** Module 08

Defense first, deliberately. Partner establishes outside ashi; you learn to
**clear the inside heel, hide the heel, and extract to the outside** before you
ever learn to finish. Then reverse roles and drill the entry to outside ashi and
the straight ankle lock only.

- **Cue:** *Hide the heel, then leave.* If you can't remove your leg, you have no business attacking theirs.
- **Safety:** rotational attacks (heel hook, toe hold) are **not** in this drill. Slow, coach-supervised, defense-literate students only. See [`jujitsu.md` Part XII](../../jujitsu.md#legality-by-belt-ibjjf-adult).
- **Detail pages:** [straight ankle lock](../../techniques/submissions/straight-ankle-lock.md) · [heel hook](../../techniques/submissions/heel-hook.md)

## P-16 · Uchikomi with breakfalls

**Atlas:** [Judo throws → *Osoto · Ouchi · Seoi nage*](../../jujitsu.md#iiib--judo-throws) · [Solo movements → *Breakfall (side and back)*](../../jujitsu.md#part-xi--solo-movements-the-physical-alphabet)

**Trains:** throw entries and safe landings together · **Dose:** 3 × 10 entries, then 10 full throws each · **Feeds:** Module 09

Ten entries without the throw (uchikomi) to groove the turn-in, then ten full
throws onto a crash pad if you have one. Rotate: **osoto gari**, **ouchi gari**,
**seoi nage** or **koshi guruma**, plus the **ankle pick** as the low-risk
alternative.

- **Cue:** *Break their posture before you turn in.* An uchikomi that starts with them standing straight is a dance step, not a throw.
- **Safety:** whoever is being thrown owns their own breakfall (S-06). No throws until breakfalls are automatic.

## P-17 · Wall and cage stand-ups

**Atlas:** [Takedown defense → *Cage / wall wrestling*](../../jujitsu.md#part-iv--takedown-defense)

**Trains:** getting up under pressure · **Dose:** 8 each side · **Feeds:** Module 10

Back to the wall, seated, partner pressuring. Back flat against the wall,
underhooks or frames, wedge a knee in, slide up the wall, **never turn away.**
Finish in a stance or in a bodylock of your own.

- **Cue:** *Up the wall, facing them.* Turning to face the wall is how the back gets taken.
- **Note:** the MMA version, under strikes, is in [`training-plan.md`](../../training-plan.md).

## P-18 · Tap timing

**Atlas:** [Concepts → *The tap*](../../jujitsu.md#part-x--the-concepts-more-important-than-any-move) · [Tier 0 — before you touch anybody → *How to tap*](../../jujitsu.md#tier-0--before-you-touch-anybody-first-10-minutes-day-one)

**Trains:** the safety system the whole sport rests on · **Dose:** once in a beginner's first week, then whenever a new person joins · **Feeds:** Module 00

Partner applies a submission slowly — armbar and RNC are the two to use — and
counts out loud from one. The drill is to tap at **two**, not at four. Then swap
and practice the release: the moment you feel the tap, you let go completely and
sit back.

- **Cue:** *Tapping is a skill, and releasing fast is the higher one.*
- **Note:** joints get tapped early, always. Neck cranks and compressions hurt before they're dangerous, but never gamble on a heel hook — there is no warning before a ligament goes.
