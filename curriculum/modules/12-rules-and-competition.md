## Why this module exists

A ruleset is not paperwork — it's the physics of the match. Guard pulling is
free in IBJJF and penalised in ADCC. Heel hooks are a career-ender at one event
and legal at the next mat over. An advantage you didn't know existed decides your
division.

You cannot play a game whose scoring you don't know.

---

## Lesson 12.1 — Points, advantages, penalties

**Atlas:** [XII — IBJJF points](../../jujitsu.md#ibjjf-points)

| Action | Points | Notes |
|---|:-:|---|
| Takedown | **2** | Must land in guard or better and hold 3 seconds |
| Sweep | **2** | Reverse from guard, stabilise 3 seconds |
| Knee-on-belly | **2** | Held 3 seconds |
| Guard pass | **3** | Clear the legs, establish control 3 seconds |
| Mount | **4** | Held 3 seconds |
| Back control (both hooks or body triangle) | **4** | Held 3 seconds |
| **Advantage** | — | A scoring action that nearly happened. Breaks ties. |
| **Penalty** | — | Stalling, fleeing the mat, illegal grips. Four = disqualification. |

**The three-second rule is the whole module in one line.** Nearly every point
lost by a beginner is a position they had and didn't hold long enough to be
given.

## Lesson 12.2 — What's legal at your belt

**Atlas:** [XII — legality by belt](../../jujitsu.md#legality-by-belt-ibjjf-adult) · [XV.c — deliberately omitted techniques](../../jujitsu.md#xvc--techniques-the-tables-omitted-on-purpose)

Full table: [`jujitsu.md` legality by belt](../../jujitsu.md#legality-by-belt-ibjjf-adult).
The short version for adults under IBJJF:

- **White:** straight ankle lock is the only leg lock. No wrist locks.
- **Blue and purple:** add wrist locks. Still no kneebar, toe hold, slicer or heel hook.
- **Brown and black:** kneebar, toe hold, slicer, and heel hooks **no-gi only**.
- **Nobody, ever:** slams, spinal locks, neck cranks, heel hooks in the gi, small-joint manipulation.

> Rules change. Check the current official ruleset for the specific event before
> you compete. This is a study aid, not a legal document.

## Lesson 12.3 — How the major rulesets differ

**Atlas:** [XII — how the major rulesets differ](../../jujitsu.md#how-the-major-rulesets-differ)

| Ruleset | Character | What it changes about your game |
|---|---|---|
| **IBJJF** | Points, time limits, gi and no-gi | Belt-based submission restrictions; guard pulling is legal and common |
| **ADCC** | No-gi, two-period | No points in the first half; guard pulling is penalised — takedowns matter |
| **EBI / submission-only** | No points at all | Overtime from set positions. Rewards finishers, punishes stallers |
| **Combat jiu-jitsu** | Grappling plus open-palm strikes | Bridges BJJ and MMA |
| **MMA / self-defense** | No rules, strikes present | Closed guard, posture and standing up become vital; inverted guard becomes a bad idea |

## Lesson 12.4 — Competition day

**Atlas:** [XIII Tier 8 — ruleset and competition](../../jujitsu.md#tier-8--ruleset-and-competition-before-the-first-tournament) · [XII — belt progression](../../jujitsu.md#belt-progression-rough-reality)

| Item | What to know |
|---|---|
| **Weigh-ins** | When, and whether it's in the gi. Some events weigh you in your gi, minutes before the match. |
| **Gi check** | Sleeve and trouser length, patch placement, no rips. Bring a spare gi if you can. |
| **Warm-up** | At the venue, 20 minutes before your division, not two hours early. |
| **Bracket reading** | Know how many matches you might have and how long between them. |
| **Stalling** | Learn what it looks like to a referee. Holding a position you're not advancing from is a penalty, not a strategy. |
| **Losing gracefully** | Shake hands, no excuses to the referee, watch the rest of your bracket. |

## Blending it into a roll

- "Every position you reach, hold it three seconds before you move on. Count out loud."
- "Score the round yourself as you go, and compare with your partner afterwards."
- "One round where the only scoring is IBJJF points — no submissions count."
- "Referee two of your teammates' rounds and explain every call."

---

## Checkpoint

- [ ] Names every scoring action and its point value without looking
- [ ] Knows exactly which submissions are legal at their belt, and at the belt above
- [ ] Referees a 5-minute match and justifies each call
- [ ] Holds every position for 3 seconds before advancing, as a habit
- [ ] Can explain how their game would change under ADCC rules
