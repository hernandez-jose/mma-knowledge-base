## Why this module exists

Beginners think a pin is a hold. It isn't — it's a **sequence of holds**, each one
chosen because of the way the person underneath just moved. A student who
understands that stops "losing" side control and starts collecting mount, back
and north–south for free.

This is also where the sport's organising principle becomes concrete:
*position before submission*. You climb, you stabilise, and then you attack.

---

## The ladder, and what pays

| Value | Position | Points (IBJJF) | What it opens |
|:-:|---|:-:|---|
| **+4** | Back control | 4 | RNC, bow and arrow, armbar — the highest finish rate in the sport |
| **+3** | Mount | 4 | Arm triangle, armbar, americana, cross collar, ezekiel |
| **+2** | Side control / KOB / north–south | 2 (KOB) | Kimura, americana, arm triangle, north–south choke, D'Arce |
| **+1** | Half guard top | — | Pass to +2, or attack the far arm |

---

## What you must own

| Position | Control details | Attacks it feeds |
|---|---|---|
| **Side control** | Crossface, underhook or far-arm block, hips low and off-centre, chest on their sternum | Kimura, americana, arm triangle, mount, back |
| **Mount** | Knees high under the armpits, hips low, head over their chest | Arm triangle, armbar, cross collar, americana |
| **High mount / S-mount** | Ride up past the elbows so they can't frame | Armbar country |
| **Knee-on-belly** | Shin across the belt line, hips up, posture tall, far hand ready | Baseball choke, armbar, transition to mount |
| **North–south** | Chest on their chest, hips over their head, arms trapping the shoulders | North–south choke, kimura, D'Arce |
| **Back control** | Seatbelt, both hooks or a body triangle, chest glued, head off-line | RNC, bow and arrow, armbar |
| **Turtle top** | Seatbelt-side ride, hips heavy, one hook working | Back take, front headlock, mat return |

## Blending it into a roll

Tasks for [R-02](../drills/integration-rolls.md#r-02--task-roll):

- "Reach mount three times. You don't have to keep it — reaching it counts."
- "No submissions until you've held a pin for 10 seconds."
- "Call the rung out loud every time you improve position."
- "Every time they turn away from you, you must take the back."
- "Transition pins at least three times per round — no camping in side control."

---

## Checkpoint

- [ ] Holds side control, mount and back for 20 s each against 70%
- [ ] Rides the pin ladder through four positions without losing control
- [ ] Takes the back from turtle and from technical mount
- [ ] Applies weight through the partner rather than posting on the mat
- [ ] Can name the trigger for each transition rather than choosing at random
