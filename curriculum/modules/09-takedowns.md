## Why this module exists

Every match, every fight and every self-defense situation starts standing. Most
BJJ students are hopeless there, because takedowns are tiring, land people hard,
and cost mat time that could be spent on guard.

So the trade is this: **short, frequent, and safe.** Twelve minutes at the start
of every class, on fresh legs, with a bias toward the entries and defenses that
don't hurt anybody. Never a forty-minute throwing session at the end of class
when everyone's tired — that's how knees get wrecked.

## What you must own

### Standing skills, before any takedown

| Skill | Drill | Why |
|---|:-:|---|
| Stance and motion | [S-13](../drills/solo-drills.md#s-13--shadow-wrestling) | Everything else stands on it |
| Grip fighting / hand fighting | [P-02](../drills/partner-drills.md#p-02--grip-fight-isolation), [G-09](../drills/positional-games.md#g-09--grips-only) | The exchange that decides the exchange |
| Collar tie and pummelling | [P-01](../drills/partner-drills.md#p-01--pummelling) | Inside control is the whole clinch |
| Level change and penetration step | [S-11](../drills/solo-drills.md#s-11--penetration-step-reps), [P-04](../drills/partner-drills.md#p-04--penetration-step-on-a-moving-partner) | Every leg attack |
| Breakfalls | [S-06](../drills/solo-drills.md#s-06--breakfall-ladder) | The prerequisite to being thrown |

### The takedowns worth owning first

| Takedown | Family | Diff | Why it's early |
|---|---|:-:|---|
| **Double leg** | Wrestling | 3 | The highest-percentage attack in grappling |
| **High single / single leg** | Wrestling | 2 | Works when the double doesn't; safest to finish |
| **Ankle pick** | Wrestling | 2 | Low risk, low commitment, huge in the gi |
| **Snap-down / front headlock** | Wrestling | 2 | Punishes their entry; the hub of [Module 10](10-scrambles.md) |
| **Bodylock** | Clinch | 3 | The MMA and self-defense answer |
| **Osoto gari** | Judo | 2 | The first throw everyone should own |
| **Ouchi gari** | Judo | 2 | Pairs with osoto; opposite direction, same grip |
| **Seoi nage / koshi guruma** | Judo | 3 | Later. High skill, high consequence for the person landing. |

### Takedown defense — taught in the same session as the takedown

| Defense | Against | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Sprawl and crossface** | Any leg attack | Hips punch the mat, then **circle** — a sprawl that stops in front is half a defense |
| **Whizzer** | Single leg | Overhook plus hips back |
| **Limp leg / re-shot** | Single leg | Best when they've committed low |
| **Front headlock** | A failed shot | Turns their defense into your offense |
| **Grip denial** | Judo entries | The gi defense that costs nothing |
| **The guard pull, done well** | Anything | A *choice*, made deliberately, with a grip — not a surrender |

## Blending it into a roll

Tasks for [R-02](../drills/integration-rolls.md#r-02--task-roll):

- "Every round starts standing. No guard pulling from either of you."
- "You may pull guard, but only after you've made one real attempt."
- "Score for getting behind them. No finish required."
- "One round where the only thing that counts is a completed takedown."
- "Every failed shot must end in a front headlock or a sit-out, never on your knees."

## Checkpoint

- [ ] Ten clean penetration steps with the head never dropping below the hips
- [ ] Three different takedowns landed against 70% defense
- [ ] Sprawl-and-circle automatic against a live shot
- [ ] Wins the grip exchange against a same-level partner more often than they lose it
- [ ] Falls safely from a throw without thinking about it

---

## Safety

- **Breakfalls before throws.** Whoever is being thrown owns their own landing.
- Takedown work goes **early in the session**, on fresh legs. Tired legs make bad shots and bad landings.
- **Never drill throws on hard mats.** Use crash pads for the judo block, or use the touch-and-go variation.
- **No slams, no spiking, no suplexes** — banned in nearly every ruleset and capable of causing spinal injury. See [`jujitsu.md` XV.c](../../jujitsu.md#xvc--techniques-the-tables-omitted-on-purpose).
