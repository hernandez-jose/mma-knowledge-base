## Why this module exists

Scrambles can't be demonstrated cleanly, so they never get taught. Turtle looks
like losing, so gyms skip it. The front headlock came from wrestling, so BJJ
treats it as optional.

All three are the same gap: **the transitional space between positions.** It is
where most matches are decided, where most beginners donate their backs, and
where a small amount of deliberate training produces an absurd return.

---

## The three subjects

### 1. The scramble

A scramble is decided in the **first three seconds**, and the rules are short:

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **Get to your feet or get behind them** | Those are the only two winning exits. Everything else is a coin flip. |
| **Face them** | Turning away is how backs are given, in every scramble, every time. |
| **Hips before hands** | Reaching with the arms in a scramble is how you get front-headlocked. |
| **Decide instantly** | A scramble you think about is a scramble you lost. Pick an exit in the first beat and commit. |
| **The loser of the scramble should end in turtle, not on their back** | Turtle is recoverable. Flat is not. |

### 2. Turtle — both sides

Turtle is a bus stop, not a house. **Exits:** sit-out ([S-08](../drills/solo-drills.md#s-08--sit-through--hip-heist)),
technical stand-up, granby roll. **Attacks against it:** the seatbelt-side ride,
hook insertion, the mat return, and the front headlock.

Untrained, turtle is a free back-take donation for your entire white-belt career.
Trained, it's a legitimate defensive position and a launching pad.

Deep page: [turtle-escapes.md](../../techniques/escapes/turtle-escapes.md)

### 3. The front headlock system

The hub that connects takedown defense, three chokes and back takes. Enormous
return on investment, and it starts from the thing that happens every time
somebody's takedown fails.

| Their reaction | Your answer |
|---|---|
| Stays low and square | **Guillotine** |
| Drives the far arm in | **D'Arce** |
| Keeps the near arm in | **Anaconda** |
| Stands up | **Back take** |
| Retreats their hips | **Go-behind** |

Drills: [P-14](../drills/partner-drills.md#p-14--the-choke-map-circuit), [C-08](../drills/chain-drills.md#c-08--snap-down--front-headlock--finish), [G-07](../drills/positional-games.md#g-07--front-headlock-game)

## Blending it into a roll

Tasks for [R-02](../drills/integration-rolls.md#r-02--task-roll):

- "Every restart begins from a broken position, chosen by your partner."
- "You may not settle. If the position stabilises for more than 10 seconds, reset."
- "Every scramble must end with you facing them or behind them — never flat."
- "Turtle deliberately three times this round and get out three different ways."
- "Every time their takedown fails, take the front headlock before anything else."

---

## Checkpoint

- [ ] Exits turtle three ways against an active rider
- [ ] Reaches a front headlock from a defended shot, and takes the correct exit for their reaction
- [ ] Ends scrambles facing or behind the opponent at least half the time
- [ ] Never turns away to escape — checked over a full round by a watching partner
- [ ] Takes the back from turtle against 70% resistance
