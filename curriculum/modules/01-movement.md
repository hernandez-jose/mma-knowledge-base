## Why this module exists

A student who cannot shrimp cannot escape. A student who cannot bridge cannot
displace. A student who cannot breakfall will not be here in a year. These are
not warm-up filler — they are the substrate, and a beginner who spends four
weeks getting them clean learns every subsequent technique roughly twice as fast.

They're also the only part of jiu-jitsu you can practice alone, which makes them
the highest-return thing to do on a rest day or in a hotel room.

## Lessons

| # | Lesson | Content |
|:-:|---|---|
| **1.1** | Hips: shrimp and reverse shrimp | Hips travel, shoulders stay. Land on your side, never flat. |
| **1.2** | Bridge and the upa | Heels close, drive *into* them rather than straight up, finish over one shoulder. |
| **1.3** | Getting up: technical stand-up | Post hand and opposite foot, kick through, never turn your back. |
| **1.4** | Falling: breakfalls and shoulder rolls | Slap and exhale on the same beat, chin tucked, over the shoulder never the head. |
| **1.5** | Standing: stance, sprawl, penetration step | Hips punch the mat first. Level change with the knees, not the waist. |
| **1.6** | Turning: sit-through and hip heist | Post, kick through, face them — before you rise onto your knees. |
| **1.7** | Advanced (month 2+): granby and inversion | Weight on the shoulder blade, never the crown of the head. Volume, not intensity. |

## The drill block

Three or four solo drills chosen to rehearse the day's technique **is** the
warm-up. Never do a generic warm-up when a specific one costs the same:

| Session focus | Warm-up |
|---|---|
| Escapes | S-01, S-03, S-04, S-15 |
| Guard | S-01, S-02, S-12, S-09 |
| Passing | S-02, S-08, S-12 |
| Takedowns | S-06, S-07, S-11, S-13 |
| Scrambles | S-04, S-05, S-08, S-07 |

**Home practice:** ten minutes, three times a week. S-01, S-03, S-04, S-12, plus
[S-14 prehab](../drills/solo-drills.md#s-14--neck-grip-and-hip-prehab).

---

## Blending it into a roll

Movement doesn't get its own roll — it gets *audited* in every roll:

- "Every time you're flat on your back today, you must shrimp before you do anything else."
- "You may not stand up from the mat except with a technical stand-up."
- "Every failed shot ends in a sit-out, not on your knees."
- One round of [R-01 flow rolling](../drills/integration-rolls.md#r-01--flow-roll) with the only instruction: *never be flat.*

---

## Checkpoint

- [ ] Two lengths of shrimps each direction, hips travelling and shoulders quiet
- [ ] Ten technical stand-ups, alternating sides, ending in a stance every time
- [ ] Side and back breakfalls automatic — slap and exhale together, head never touches
- [ ] Ten penetration steps with the head never dropping below the hips
- [ ] Sprawl-and-circle reflex on a partner's 60% shot
- [ ] Shoulder rolls both directions with no neck loading
