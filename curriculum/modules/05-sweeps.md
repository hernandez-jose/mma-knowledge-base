## Why this module exists

A sweep is worth two points and a complete change of the fight. Wrestling up is
worth the same, is the highest-scoring bottom action in modern no-gi, and is the
only correct answer in a self-defense context: **get up.**

BJJ culture assumes bottom means guard, not standing. That assumption costs
students years.

---

## The mechanical rule underneath every sweep

> **A sweep removes a base point, then moves them across the gap where it was.**

Not "push them over." Every failed sweep in the gym is somebody trying to move a
person who still has three points of base on the mat. The sequence is always:

1. **Off-balance** (kuzushi) — break their posture or their weight distribution.
2. **Kill the post** — the hand or knee they'd catch themselves with.
3. **Move them across that gap** — with a hook, a lever, or your own hips.
4. **Follow them up** — a sweep that ends with you still on the bottom is a shove.

## What you must own

| Sweep | From | Diff | When it's the right one |
|---|---|:-:|---|
| **Scissor** | Closed guard | 1 | They're upright, knees together |
| **Hip bump** | Closed guard | 1 | They post a hand back |
| **Flower / pendulum** | Closed guard | 2 | Their arm is across, weight forward |
| **Kimura sweep** | Closed guard | 2 | They post the hand you just gripped |
| **Butterfly elevator** | Butterfly | 2 | Chest connection plus an underhook |
| **Arm drag to back** | Butterfly / seated | 3 | They post the far arm out |
| **Old school** | Half guard | 2 | You have the underhook and their far ankle |
| **Tripod / sickle** | Open guard | 1–2 | Feet on hips, they stand to pass |
| **Wrestle-up** | Seated / half | 3 | Anytime, and more often than you think |
| **Technical stand-up** | Anywhere on bottom | 1 | Whenever they're not attached to you |

## Blending it into a roll

Tasks for [R-02](../drills/integration-rolls.md#r-02--task-roll):

- "Every round starts with you seated in front of a standing partner."
- "You may not submit anyone from the bottom — you must come up first."
- "Get to your feet twice this round, however you like."
- "Sweep with the same sweep three times. Once they know it's coming, that's the real rep."
- "Every time you sweep, keep going to a pin — a sweep that stops at neutral doesn't count."

---

## Checkpoint

- [ ] Sweeps at 70% resistance from closed guard, using at least two different sweeps
- [ ] Butterfly elevator or arm drag lands against an actively based partner
- [ ] Comes up to standing from seated guard against a 70% opponent
- [ ] Follows every sweep to a stabilised top position, not just to neutral
- [ ] Can explain which post each of their sweeps removes
