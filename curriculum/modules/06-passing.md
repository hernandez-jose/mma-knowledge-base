## Why this module exists

Passing is the slowest skill to acquire because it's the only one where the
other person gets to use four limbs against your two. It's also where most
students plateau: they learn six passes as isolated tricks, none of which connect,
and get stuck at the same place every roll.

The fix is structural. **Passing is three decisions, not thirty techniques:**

1. **Which side of their hips am I on?**
2. **Are their legs between us, or beside us?**
3. **Standing or on my knees?**

Every pass in the atlas is an answer to those three.

---

## The two families

| Family | Idea | Passes | Best against |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pressure / smash** | Take their space away, make them carry your weight, walk through | Knee cut, body lock, over-under, smash pass, stack | Flexible, mobile, active guards |
| **Speed / distance** | Never let them attach, move around the legs | Toreando, leg drag, long step, floating pass | Grip-heavy guards, bigger opponents |

Own **one pass from each family.** Knee cut and toreando is the classic pairing
and covers most of the sport. If you only pressure-pass, an athletic guard player
will out-move you; if you only speed-pass, a heavy one will hold you in place.

---

## What you must own

| Pass | Family | Diff | Notes |
|---|---|:-:|---|
| **Opening the closed guard** (standing and kneeling) | — | 2 | Prerequisite to everything. Standing is safer with strikes present. |
| **Knee cut** | Pressure | 2 | Underhook + crossface, slice the knee across, hip to the mat. |
| **Toreando** | Speed | 2 | Pin the legs to one side, walk around, land on the hip. |
| **Body lock pass** | Pressure | 3 | Clasp low, chest heavy, walk the legs down. Enormous against bigger guards. |
| **Leg drag** | Speed | 3 | Drag the top leg across your centre; the back becomes available. |
| **Over-under** | Pressure | 3 | Slow, heavy, and nearly un-scrambleable once set. |
| **Half guard pass** (underhook + crossface) | Pressure | 2 | Where most passes actually end up. |
| **Headquarters** | Hub | 3 | Not a pass — the position all of the above launch from. |

## Blending it into a roll

Tasks for [R-02](../drills/integration-rolls.md#r-02--task-roll):

- "Pass with one named pass only, all round."
- "You may not submit until you've passed and held for three seconds."
- "Every pass must start from headquarters."
- "If you get swept, you must pass again before the round counts anything."
- "Pass standing only — no knee passing at all."

---

## Checkpoint

- [ ] Opens a closed guard standing, against 70% resistance
- [ ] Passes three different ways against a 70% guard, holding each pin 3 s
- [ ] Owns one pressure pass and one speed pass, and can say when each applies
- [ ] Chains two passes together when the first is defended
- [ ] Passes half guard against an active knee shield

---

## Common errors

| Error | Fix |
|---|---|
| Passing the legs instead of the hips | Legs move. Hips are the position. Control the hip line and the legs follow. |
| Standing up inside a closed guard without posture | Grips and posture first, then stand. Otherwise you're being swept, slowly. |
| Six passes, none connected | Two passes and a chain beat six passes and a shrug. |
| Rushing past into a scramble | A pass isn't finished until you've held it 3 seconds. Land, then breathe, then move. |
| Passing with the arms | Your chest and hips pass. Your arms just tidy up. |
