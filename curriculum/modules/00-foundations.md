## Why this module exists

The most common injuries in jiu-jitsu are not caused by techniques. They are
caused by a student who didn't know how to tap, a partner who didn't know how to
release, long fingernails, an infection somebody trained through, and an ego that
turned a drill into a fight.

None of that is technique, so almost no syllabus gives it a slot. It gets ten
minutes here, before anything else, and then it gets repeated — because the
person who needs it most is always the one who joined last week.

---

## What you must own

1. **The tap** — two clear taps on their body, or on the mat, or the word "tap".
   Loud, early, without ego.
2. **The release** — instant, complete, and without asking whether they meant it.
3. **Etiquette** — bow or shake hands, no shoes on the mat, ask before joining a
   roll, never walk through someone else's round.
4. **Hygiene** — gi washed after *every* session, nails short, shower before and
   after, cover open cuts, no jewellery, don't train with an infection.
5. **Injury communication** — before the round, not during it.
6. **How to roll with a body that isn't yours** — see [R-12](../drills/integration-rolls.md#r-12--body-mismatch-roll).
7. **Breathing** — nasal, rhythmic, continuous. Especially when it's hard.

## Blending it into a roll

Tasks for [R-02](../drills/integration-rolls.md#r-02--task-roll):

- "Call your percentage out loud before every round today."
- "Tap three times this session on purpose, early, before it's tight."
- "Roll one round with someone 20 kg heavier and one with someone 20 kg lighter. Different job each time."
- "Nasal breathing only for one full round."

---

## Checkpoint

- [ ] Taps at a count of two, every time, on joints
- [ ] Releases instantly on a tap, including a verbal one
- [ ] Nails short, gi clean, no jewellery — checked, not assumed
- [ ] States existing injuries before a round without being asked
- [ ] Completes 90 s under a heavier partner breathing through the nose
- [ ] Can say what changes when rolling with someone much smaller
