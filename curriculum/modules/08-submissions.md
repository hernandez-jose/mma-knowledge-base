## Why the order is chokes → arms → legs

| Order | Family | Why here |
|:-:|---|---|
| **1** | **Chokes** | Highest finishing percentage in competition, lowest injury risk to your partner, and they work regardless of how tough someone is. A choke doesn't care about pain tolerance. |
| **2** | **Arm and shoulder locks** | Higher availability, more escapes, and a real injury cost if applied fast. Learn them once you can hold a position. |
| **3** | **Leg locks** | Highest injury risk, least warning before damage, most ruleset restrictions. **Defense first** ([Module 03](03-submission-defense.md)), then the straight ankle lock, then entanglement positions, then rotational attacks — slowly, and with a coach. |
| **4** | **Chains** | Because above white belt, nothing works in isolation. |

## Blending it into a roll

Tasks for [R-02](../drills/integration-rolls.md#r-02--task-roll):

- "Chokes only. No joint locks at all this round."
- "Every attack must be a response to their defense of the last one — no repeats."
- "Catch and release: lock it, feel it, let it go, keep rolling."
- "Announce the submission before you apply it. If they still can't stop it, it's yours."
- "One submission for the whole round, from as many positions as you can find."

---

## Checkpoint

- [ ] Finishes the RNC against an actively hand-fighting partner at 70%
- [ ] Hits the armbar from at least three different positions
- [ ] Runs the front headlock map: snap-down → headlock → correct finish for their reaction
- [ ] Chains three submissions catch-and-release without losing position
- [ ] Applies every submission slowly enough that the partner's tap always lands first

---

## Safety

- **Apply slowly. Release instantly.** Speed adds nothing to a submission except injuries.
- **Leg locks:** straight ankle lock only until a coach clears you. Rotational attacks are drilled at a crawl. There is little warning before a knee ligament tears.
- **Never crank the neck.** Twisters, can openers and neck cranks are outside this curriculum.
- **Know your ruleset** before you compete: [`jujitsu.md` legality by belt](../../jujitsu.md#legality-by-belt-ibjjf-adult).
