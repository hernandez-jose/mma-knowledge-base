## The problem this solves

Most curricula are a list of techniques. A list is not a syllabus, for three
reasons:

1. **It has no order.** Techniques get taught in the order the coach finds them
   interesting, so students meet the armbar before they can escape mount.
2. **It has no drills.** A technique shown is not a technique owned, and the
   distance between the two is measured in reps and games, not in demonstrations.
3. **It never reaches the roll.** Class teaches a technique, everybody drills it
   twenty times, the whistle goes, everybody plays their existing game, and the
   material is gone within a fortnight.

So this curriculum is built as three connected things, and the third one is the
point:

```
   MODULE  ──▶  DRILL BLOCK  ──▶  INTEGRATION ROLL
   what to       reps, chains,     the constraint that forces
   learn         and games         it into live sparring
```

Every module ends with a **"blending it into a roll"** section: the specific
tasks to hand a student so that today's material appears in tonight's rounds
instead of evaporating.

so a "Live" block points at a real, named format rather than at nothing.

## The session template

Sixty minutes, built from one module's drill block:

| Minutes | Block | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8 | Warm-up — solo movements chosen for today's technique | [solo-drills.md](drills/solo-drills.md) |
| 8–13 | Review of last session, 2 min explanation + 3 min drill | previous module lesson |
| 13–20 | New technique, taught in layers — **one or two, never six** | module lesson |
| 20–32 | Reps, cooperative then resistant, plus a chain | [partner](drills/partner-drills.md) + [chain drills](drills/chain-drills.md) |
| 32–45 | Positional game from today's exact position | [positional-games.md](drills/positional-games.md) |
| 45–58 | Roll — one integration format, with today's task | [integration-rolls.md](drills/integration-rolls.md) |
| 58–60 | Three-line notes | [R-15](drills/integration-rolls.md#r-15--debrief-roll) |

**Ninety minutes:** add a 10-minute standing block and a 10-minute escape block
before the roll. These are the two things that get cut when time is short, and
they are the two things students need most.

## The 16-week cycle

One full pass through the curriculum. Repeat it — the second pass means something
completely different from the first.

| Week | Focus | Module | Concept of the week | Checkpoint |
|:-:|---|:-:|---|---|
| **1** | Foundations and movement | 00, 01 | The tap | Ten movements clean |
| **2** | Mount and side control escapes | 02 | Frames | Escape both at 50% |
| **3** | Back defense and turtle | 02, 10 | Hip escape | Escape back at 50% |
| **4** | Submission defense | 03 | Breathing | Early defense × 6 attacks |
| **5** | Standing: stance, grips, sprawl | 09 | Grip fighting | Sprawl-and-circle automatic |
| **6** | Takedowns: double, single, ankle pick | 09 | Distance management | One takedown at 50% |
| **7** | Closed guard and retention | 04 | Posture | Retain 60 s vs 50% passer |
| **8** | Half guard, both chairs | 04, 06 | Base | Knee shield within 5 s |
| **9** | Sweeps from closed and butterfly | 05 | Timing over strength | Sweep at 70% |
| **10** | Wrestling up | 05, 09 | The scramble | Stand up from seated vs 70% |
| **11** | Passing: knee cut and toreando | 06 | Connection / heaviness | Pass at 50%, two ways |
| **12** | Passing chains and half guard passing | 06 | Chaining | Pass at 70%, three ways |
| **13** | Pins and transitions | 07 | Position before submission | Hold any pin 20 s at 70% |
| **14** | Chokes: RNC, triangle, arm triangle, guillotine | 08 | Position before submission | Finish RNC vs active defense |
| **15** | Arm locks and the front headlock hub | 08, 10 | Chaining | Run the choke map |
| **16** | Legs (defense first), rules, and the test | 08, 12 | The tap | [The 15-item test](#how-to-know-it-worked) |

**Running alongside, every week:** five minutes of grip fighting
([G-09](drills/positional-games.md#g-09--grips-only)), one scramble game
([G-10](drills/positional-games.md#g-10--scramble-start)), and three-line notes
after every session ([R-15](drills/integration-rolls.md#r-15--debrief-roll)).

**Modules 11 and 13 are never scheduled** — 11 supplies the concept of the week,
and 13 is a set of habits reviewed once a quarter.

## Blending it all into a roll

The whole curriculum exists to survive this transition, and
[integration-rolls.md](drills/integration-rolls.md) is where it happens. The
four-week arc for any technique:

| Week | Format | Constraint | You should see |
|:-:|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Task roll](drills/integration-rolls.md#r-02--task-roll) | Heavy | It appears because you forced it |
| 2 | [Chain](drills/integration-rolls.md#r-05--chain-roll) or [restart roll](drills/integration-rolls.md#r-04--positional-restart-roll) | Medium | It appears when the position is handed to you |
| 3 | Open rolling, one task kept | Light | It appears two or three times unprompted |
| 4 | Open rolling, no constraint | None | It appears when you weren't thinking about it — **it's yours** |

If week 4 arrives and the technique never shows up on its own, it hasn't been
learned — it's been demonstrated. Go back to week 1 with it, or drop it and pick
something that suits your game. Both are honest. Pretending is not.

## How to know it worked

The 15-technique test, taken straight from
[`jujitsu.md` XV.d](../jujitsu.md#xvd--the-honest-final-gap): a student who
genuinely owns these beats a student who can name three hundred.

- [ ] Upa (trap-and-roll) — [Module 02](modules/02-survival.md)
- [ ] Frame-and-shrimp from side control — [Module 02](modules/02-survival.md)
- [ ] Back escape — [Module 02](modules/02-survival.md)
- [ ] Closed guard: hold it and break their posture — [Module 04](modules/04-guard.md)
- [ ] Knee shield half guard — [Module 04](modules/04-guard.md)
- [ ] Scissor sweep — [Module 05](modules/05-sweeps.md)
- [ ] Butterfly sweep — [Module 05](modules/05-sweeps.md)
- [ ] Knee cut pass — [Module 06](modules/06-passing.md)
- [ ] Toreando pass — [Module 06](modules/06-passing.md)
- [ ] Rear naked choke — [Module 08](modules/08-submissions.md)
- [ ] Armbar — [Module 08](modules/08-submissions.md)
- [ ] Triangle — [Module 08](modules/08-submissions.md)
- [ ] Arm triangle — [Module 08](modules/08-submissions.md)
- [ ] Guillotine — [Module 08](modules/08-submissions.md)
- [ ] Straight ankle lock — [Module 08](modules/08-submissions.md)

**Own** means: it works at 70% resistance, on both sides, against someone who
knows it's coming, and it appears in an unconstrained roll without you planning
it.

> **Depth beats breadth. Escapes beat submissions. Chokes beat joint locks.
> Position beats everything.**
