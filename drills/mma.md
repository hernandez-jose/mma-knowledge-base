# MMA — Cross-Art Combinations and Drills

Hub: [`mma.md`](../mma.md) · Notation and safety rules: [`README.md`](README.md). These combine striking, takedown and ground skills under MMA-style rules (gloves are smaller than boxing gloves, the cage is a factor). **Diff** uses the atlas scale. Tokens beyond the striking ones: `lvl` = level change, `dl` = double leg, `sl-leg` = single leg, `spr` = sprawl, `gnp` = ground strike (controlled), `gu` = get-up.

## 2. Cross-art partner drills

| # | Name | Roles | Rules | Intensity | Timing | Goal |
|:-:|---|---|---|:-:|---|---|
| XD1 | **Strike-to-shot reps** | Shooter / receiver | Shooter throws 1-2 at half speed, then a level change into a double leg to a pad or a mat; receiver sprawls and resets. | 2 | 6 × 1 min | Setups |
| XD2 | **Sprawl and brawl** | Shooter / sprawler | Shooter shoots; sprawler sprawls, steps out and throws 1-2 at the shooter's pad. | 2 | 6 × 1 min | Takedown defense with a counter |
| XD3 | **Clinch to takedown** | Equal partners | Start in a collar tie and underhook. Work for a trip or throw with a resisting partner at 30%. | 2→3 | 5 × 2 min | Clinch work |
| XD4 | **Cage wall drill** | Presser / defender | Presser pins the defender on the cage. Defender frames, pivots or takes an underhook to turn. | 2 | 5 × 1 min | Cage escapes |
| XD5 | **Get-up under strikes** | Bottom / top | Bottom stands up (technical stand-up or wall walk) while the top holds a pad and taps at 10%. | 2 | 5 × 1 min | Getting up |
| XD6 | **Ground-and-pound defense** | Top / bottom | Top throws slow pad taps to the head from mount or side control; bottom frames, covers, and escapes. | 2 | 5 × 1 min | Posture and cover |
| XD7 | **Back control with strikes** | Top / bottom | Top holds hooks and a seatbelt; bottom defends hands. Strikes at touch intensity; top finishes a choke. | 2 | 5 × 90 s | Strikes plus submission |
| XD8 | **MMA rounds (progressive)** | Equal partners | Standing only → takedowns only → ground only → full. | 3→4 | 4 × 3 min | Blending the arts |

### 2.1 Safety for cross-art drills
- Gloves: 4–6 oz MMA gloves are *not* sparring gloves in the gym. Use 12–16 oz gloves, and pads for ground strikes.
- Slams and spikes are not drills; return to the mat.
- No knees or kicks to a grounded partner.
- Concussion and neck-injury protocols are the same as for boxing.
