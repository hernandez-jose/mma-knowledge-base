## 1. Combinations

### 1.1 Offensive combinations

| # | Notation | In words | Why it works | Diff |
|:-:|---|---|---|:-:|
| B1 | `1-2` | Jab, cross | The jab opens the guard and hides the cross. The foundation of everything. | 1 |
| B2 | `1-1-2` | Double jab, cross | The second jab hides the cross and resets their guard. | 2 |
| B3 | `1-2-3` | Jab, cross, lead hook | The cross pushes their head across; the hook lands on the side they have turned toward. | 2 |
| B4 | `1-2-3-2` | Jab, cross, hook, cross | Finishing cross as the hook turns them. | 3 |
| B5 | `1-3` | Jab, lead hook | Short and quick; the jab blinds, the hook arrives around the guard. | 2 |
| B6 | `1-1b-1` | Jab, jab to the body, jab | Changes level; the body jab drops their hands for the head jab. | 2 |
| B7 | `1-2-3b-3` | Jab, cross, hook to the body, hook to the head | Body-to-head: the liver shot pulls the elbow down. | 3 |
| B8 | `1-2-5-2` | Jab, cross, lead uppercut, cross | The uppercut lifts the chin or splits a high guard. | 3 |
| B9 | `2-3-2` | Cross, hook, cross | A power sequence for when they are walking forward. | 3 |
| B10 | `3-2-3` | Hook, cross, hook | Power triple; the hook turns them, the cross lands, the hook finishes. | 3 |
| B11 | `1-2-3-6-3` | Jab, cross, hook, rear uppercut, hook | The long combination; keep your hands up between shots. | 4 |
| B12 | `1b-2b-3` | Body jab, body cross, head hook | Low, low, high. | 3 |
| B13 | `1-2-pv-3` | Jab, cross, pivot, lead hook | Ends on an angle instead of in front of them. | 4 |
| B14 | `fi-1-2-3` | Feint the jab, then jab-cross-hook | Draws the guard up or the weight back before you commit. | 3 |
| B15 | `2o-3` | Overhand, lead hook | Over the top, then round the side; used against a tall or ducking opponent. | 3 |

## 2. Partner drills

All drills start at intensity 1–2 and rise only when the previous level is clean. See the ladder in the [README](README.md).

| # | Name | Roles | Rules | Intensity | Timing | Goal |
|:-:|---|---|---|:-:|---|---|
| BD1 | **Mirror footwork** | Leader / follower | Leader moves (advance, retreat, circle, pivot); follower keeps the same distance. Swap every round. | 1 | 3 × 2 min | Distance and footwork |
| BD2 | **Jab, jab-back** | A throws jabs, B defends | A jabs at a steady pace. B only parries or slips, no counter. Then B adds a return jab. | 1→2 | 3 × 2 min each | Jab defense |
| BD3 | **One-punch feed** | Feeder / counter-puncher | Feeder throws a named punch (jab, cross, hook) at a slow pace. Counter-puncher answers with the matching counter from Part VII of the atlas. | 1→3 | 6 × 1 min | Counters per punch |
| BD4 | **Slip line with a partner** | Feeder / slipper | Feeder throws a slow jab or cross at the head; slipper slips and returns to guard. Add a counter on the third rep. | 2 | 3 × 2 min | Slip timing |
| BD5 | **Catch and return** | Feeder / catcher | Feeder jabs; catcher catches in the glove and returns a jab. | 2 | 3 × 2 min | Defensive hands |
| BD6 | **Body-shot elbow block** | Feeder / defender | Feeder throws slow body hooks. Defender drops the elbow and counters with a hook to the head. | 2 | 3 × 2 min | Body defense |
| BD7 | **Roll-and-hook** | Feeder / roller | Feeder throws slow lead hooks to the head; roller rolls under and comes up with a hook. | 2 | 3 × 2 min | Rolling timing |
| BD8 | **Pummelling and tie-up** | Equal partners | Inside position battle: tie up, push off, reset. No punches. | 2 | 3 × 2 min | Clinch and inside control |
| BD9 | **Southpaw / orthodox lead-foot battle** | Equal partners | Fight for lead-foot position only (outside foot). Light jabs allowed. | 2 | 3 × 2 min | Foot control against a southpaw |
| BD10 | **One-weapon sparring: jab only** | Equal partners | Only the jab and footwork are allowed. Winner is who lands more clean jabs. | 3 | 3 × 2 min | Range and control |
| BD11 | **Ring-cutting drill** | Chaser / mover | Chaser cuts off angles; mover must circle without backing in a straight line. | 2 | 3 × 2 min | Ring generalship |
| BD12 | **Rope escape (pivot)** | Pressure / escaper | Pressure fighter pins the escaper on the ropes. Escaper pivots and slides out. | 2 | 3 × 1 min | Escape the ropes |
| BD13 | **Feint and read** | Feinter / reader | Feinter shows jab feints. Reader must stay calm (no reaction) and counter only on a real jab. | 2 | 3 × 2 min | Reading feints |
| BD14 | **Pad work (holder calls)** | Holder / boxer | Holder calls numbers (e.g. "1-2-3"); throws a counter punch back at the boxer to train defense. | 2→3 | 5 × 3 min | Combinations and defense |
| BD15 | **Combination relay** | Pair | Partner A throws a combination; partner B repeats it and adds one punch. Continue until someone breaks the sequence. | 2 | 3 × 3 min | Memory and flow |
| BD16 | **Technical sparring** | Equal partners | Light, controlled sparring; coach picks the theme (e.g. "jab and slip"). | 3–4 | 3 × 2 min | Application |

### 2.1 Progressions

- **Add speed:** from half to three-quarters.
- **Add unpredictability:** feeder chooses between two punches.
- **Add fatigue:** do the drill after a bag round.

### 2.2 Safety for boxing drills

- Head contact is intensity 2 or lower outside supervised sparring.
- Use 14–16 oz gloves for sparring in the gym (check your coach's standard).
- Mouthguard always. Stop at the first sign of concussion symptoms.

## 3. Solo practice (so partner time is not wasted)

| Drill | What to do | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Shadowboxing with a theme | One idea per round: jab, defense, body, angles | 3 × 3 min |
| Combination ladder | Throw B1, then B2, then B3 … each 10 times | 3 × 3 min |
| Slip bag / rope | Slip in a line under a rope while throwing a counter | 3 × 2 min |
| Double-end bag | Accuracy and defense | 3 × 3 min |
