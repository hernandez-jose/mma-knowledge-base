# Muay Thai — Combinations and Partner Drills

Atlas: [`muay-thai.md`](../muay-thai.md) · Notation and safety rules: [`README.md`](README.md). Written for **Thai-style rules** (elbows, knees, clinch and sweeps legal); check your event's rules before using elbow or throw content. Orthodox stance. **Diff** uses the atlas scale.

## 2. Partner drills

| # | Name | Roles | Rules | Intensity | Timing | Goal |
|:-:|---|---|---|:-:|---|---|
| MD1 | **Teep and block** | Teeper / blocker | Teeper pushes the belly; blocker lifts the lead knee or parries. Swap. | 2 | 3 × 2 min | Distance, teep defense |
| MD2 | **Teep war** | Equal partners | Only teeps. Win by scoring clean and staying balanced. | 2 | 3 × 2 min | Rhythm |
| MD3 | **Check and counter** | Kicker / checker | Slow rear low kick; checker lifts the shin, checks, and counters on the third rep. | 2→3 | 3 × 2 min | The check |
| MD4 | **Catch and sweep** | Kicker / catcher | Slow body kick; catcher traps the leg under the arm, steps and sweeps the base leg onto a mat. | 2 | 6 × 1 min | The catch |
| MD5 | **Pummelling for the plum** | Equal partners | Fight for inside hands. Reset when someone gets the collar tie. | 2 | 3 × 2 min | Clinch entry |
| MD6 | **Plum and posture** | Controller / defender | Controller holds the plum and pulls; defender keeps the chin up and spine tall. Swap each round. | 2 | 3 × 2 min | Posture |
| MD7 | **Knee to the pad** | Controller / holder | Controller holds the plum on a standing partner who holds a Thai pad or body pad at the ribs. Throw knees at 30–50% power. | 3 | 5 × 2 min | Knee technique and hip drive |
| MD8 | **Clinch sweeps (compliant)** | Sweeper / receiver | Sweeper pulls and sweeps; receiver reacts slowly and falls on a mat. Increase resistance each round. | 2→3 | 5 × 2 min | Sweeps |
| MD9 | **Clinch escape** | Controller / escaper | Controller holds a plum; escaper frames, underhooks and turns out. | 2 | 3 × 2 min | Escape |
| MD10 | **Elbow pads** | Holder / striker | Holder holds Thai pads or elbow pads; striker throws controlled elbows on call. | 2→3 | 5 × 2 min | Elbow technique |
| MD11 | **Pad work with the kru** | Holder / fighter | Holder calls combinations from Part 1 and counters back with a slow kick or knee to train checks and blocks. | 3 | 5 × 3 min | Conditioning and technique |
| MD12 | **Clinch round: knees to the body only** | Equal partners | Collar ties, knees to the body at light power; reset when the referee-coach claps. | 3 | 3 × 3 min | Clinch work |
| MD13 | **One-weapon sparring: legs and teeps** | Equal partners | Only teeps, low kicks and checks. | 3 | 3 × 2 min | Distance and defense |
| MD14 | **Technical sparring** | Equal partners | Controlled themed rounds (e.g. "teep, check, clinch"). | 3–4 | 3 × 3 min | Application |
| MD15 | **Combination relay** | Pair | One partner throws a combination; the other repeats it and adds one more step. | 2 | 3 × 3 min | Memory and flow |

### 2.1 Progressions
- Add a feint before the first strike.
- Replace the compliant sweep with a partner who resists 25%, 50%, then 75%.
- Add a punch window: the holder throws one slow punch back to train blocking.

### 2.2 Safety for Muay Thai drills
- **Elbows and head knees: intensity 2 or lower; use pads.** Elbows cut; a cut ends a training week.
- Clinch drills happen on a mat or soft floor; sweeps and dumps always have a plan for the fall.
- Shin guards for partner kicks. Hard shin-on-shin contact is not a drill.
- Never throw an opponent onto their head or slam; if the receiver does not go, reset.
- Stop at the first sign of a concussion or a hyperextended knee.

## 3. Solo practice

| Drill | What to do | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Teep on the bag | Alternate rear and lead teeps with full hip drive | 5 × 2 min |
| Kick and return | Kick the bag and recover to guard | 5 × 3 min |
| Knee on the bag | Straight and curving knees | 3 × 2 min |
| Elbow shadow | Slow elbows in the air with hip rotation | 3 × 2 min |
| Wai kru and ram muay | Practice the ritual with a teacher's guidance | 5 min |
