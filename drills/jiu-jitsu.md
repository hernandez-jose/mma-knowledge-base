# Jiu-Jitsu — Attack Chains and Partner Drills

Atlas: [`jujitsu.md`](../jujitsu.md) · Technique pages: [submissions](../techniques/submissions/) and [escapes](../techniques/escapes/) · Notation and safety rules: [`README.md`](README.md). In grappling, a "combo" is a **chain**: *if they defend A, take B*. Arrows `→` mean "when they defend, go to". **Diff** uses the atlas scale.

## 1. Attack chains

### 1.1 Submission chains

| # | Chain | Trigger (what they do) | Why it works | Diff |
|:-:|---|---|---|:-:|
| J1 | [Armbar](../techniques/submissions/armbar.md) → [Triangle](../techniques/submissions/triangle-choke.md) | They pull the arm out of the armbar | The arm that escaped is now inside your legs, with the head. | 3 |
| J2 | [Triangle](../techniques/submissions/triangle-choke.md) → [Armbar](../techniques/submissions/armbar.md) | They posture and push the knee | Their arm is straight when they push; swing the leg over. | 3 |
| J3 | [Triangle](../techniques/submissions/triangle-choke.md) → [Omoplata](../techniques/submissions/omoplata.md) | They stack or clear the head | Turn the triangle's shoulder pressure into a shoulder lock. | 4 |
| J4 | [Guillotine](../techniques/submissions/guillotine.md) → [Anaconda](../techniques/submissions/anaconda.md) / [D'Arce](../techniques/submissions/darce.md) | They pull their head out of the guillotine | The front-headlock family shares the same entry. | 3 |
| J5 | [Kimura](../techniques/submissions/kimura.md) → sweep or back take | They hug the arm or pull it into their body | Use the grip as a handle to pull them over. | 3 |
| J6 | [Americana](../techniques/submissions/americana.md) → [Armbar](../techniques/submissions/armbar.md) (from mount) | The elbow lifts away from the body | Release the lock, step the leg over the face. | 3 |
| J7 | [Arm triangle](../techniques/submissions/arm-triangle.md) → mount-to-back-control chain | They turn onto their side | Follow into a technical mount and back control. | 4 |
| J8 | [Rear naked choke](../techniques/submissions/rear-naked-choke.md) → [Bow and arrow](../techniques/submissions/bow-and-arrow.md) (gi) | They defend the neck with both hands | Switch to a collar choke with the leg lever. | 4 |
| J9 | [Heel hook](../techniques/submissions/heel-hook.md) entry → [Straight ankle lock](../techniques/submissions/straight-ankle-lock.md) | They pull the heel out | Ankle lock is a safer fallback from the same position. | 4 |

### 1.2 Closed-guard system (the "hip bump – kimura – guillotine" trio)

| Their action | Your attack |
|---|---|
| They post a hand and sit up | Hip bump sweep |
| They post a hand and stay up | [Kimura](../techniques/submissions/kimura.md) |
| They lift the head or the posture breaks forward | [Guillotine](../techniques/submissions/guillotine.md) |
| They stand up in the guard | Open-guard transitions (see atlas Part II.b) |

## 2. Partner drills

| # | Name | Roles | Rules | Intensity | Timing | Goal |
|:-:|---|---|---|:-:|---|---|
| JD1 | **Technical flow** | Flow partner A / B | Trade positions slowly: pass, sweep, reverse. No submissions. Neither partner resists more than 30%. | 2 | 3 × 5 min | Transitions |
| JD2 | **Positional sparring** | Top / bottom | Start in a named position (e.g. side control). Bottom scores by escaping; top scores by holding or advancing. Reset on the goal. | 3→4 | 6 × 2 min | A single position |
| JD3 | **Submission feeding (0→50%)** | Attacker / defender | Defender resists 0%, 25%, then 50%. The attacker repeats a chain from Part 1. | 2→3 | 6 × 90 s | Finishing |
| JD4 | **Escape-to-attack** | Top / bottom | Bottom escapes, then immediately attacks (e.g. mount escape → closed guard → armbar). | 3 | 6 × 2 min | Chaining escapes to offense |
| JD5 | **Grip fighting** | Equal partners | Gi or no-gi. Each tries to establish a grip; no takedowns. | 2→3 | 5 × 2 min | Grip control |
| JD6 | **Entry reps (uchikomi)** | Thrower / receiver | Enter a throw or takedown without finishing; receiver is stable. | 1→2 | 3 × 10 reps each | Takedown entries |
| JD7 | **Sprawl drill** | Shooter / sprawler | Shooter shoots a double leg at 50%; sprawler sprawls and takes the front headlock. | 2 | 5 × 1 min | Takedown defense |
| JD8 | **Survival rounds** | Top / bottom | Bottom survives from the worst position (back or mount) for the round; top attacks at 70% speed. | 3→4 | 5 × 2 min | Defense under pressure |
| JD9 | **Shrimp and bridge ladders** | Solo / partner | Partner provides light pressure while you shrimp and bridge. | 2 | 3 × 2 min | The physical alphabet |
| JD10 | **Guard retention** | Passer / guard player | Passer tries to pass; guard player re-guards without attacking. | 3 | 6 × 2 min | Guard recovery |
| JD11 | **King of the hill** | Group | One pair rolls; the winner stays. Loser swaps out. | 4 | 15 min | Competition-style flow |
| JD12 | **Sweep and stand** | Equal partners | Bottom sweeps and comes up to standing; top re-engages. | 3 | 5 × 2 min | Sweeps and get-ups |

### 2.1 Progressions
- Resistance increases only in 25% steps.
- After a drill, run one live round that starts from the drill's position.
- Add a time limit (60–90 s) so the attacker must chain, not just hold on.

### 2.2 Safety for jiu-jitsu drills
- **Tap early; release the instant a partner taps or says stop.**
- Joint locks: slow in, slow out. Never crank on a resisting partner.
- Leg-lock drills: heel hooks and toe holds at intensity 2 with experienced partners only; no spinning or explosive entries.
- Neck and spine: no cranks; no slams or spikes.
- Trim nails; remove jewellery; wash gi and mats.

## 3. Solo practice

| Drill | What to do | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Hip-escape and bridge | Shrimp across the mat, bridge ten times per side | 3 × 2 min |
| Technical stand-up | Alternate sides | 3 × 10 |
| Shoulder rolls | Forward and backward, never over the head | 3 × 10 |
| Sprawl and get-up | Five reps then a hip heist | 3 × 5 |
