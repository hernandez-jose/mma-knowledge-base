## 2. Partner drills

| # | Name | Roles | Rules | Intensity | Timing | Goal |
|:-:|---|---|---|:-:|---|---|
| KD1 | **Shin check drill** | Kicker / checker | Kicker throws a slow rear low kick to the lead thigh. Checker lifts the knee and meets the shin with their own; return to guard. | 2 | 3 × 2 min each | The check |
| KD2 | **Check and counter** | Kicker / checker | As above, then the checker counters with a cross or a low kick on the third rep. | 2→3 | 3 × 2 min | Check-then-counter timing |
| KD3 | **Catch and counter** | Kicker / catcher | Kicker throws a slow body kick; catcher traps it under the arm and counters with a cross. | 2 | 3 × 2 min | Catching |
| KD4 | **Elbow block (body kick)** | Kicker / blocker | Kicker throws a slow body kick; blocker drops the elbow tight to the ribs and returns a punch. | 2 | 3 × 2 min | Body-kick defense |
| KD5 | **Teep defense** | Teeper / defender | Teeper throws teeps to the belly. Defender parries, side-steps or lifts the lead knee. | 2 | 3 × 2 min | Teep defense |
| KD6 | **Teep war** | Equal partners | Only teeps allowed. Win by landing clean teeps on the body and by keeping balance. | 2→3 | 3 × 2 min | Distance control |
| KD7 | **Kick-for-kick ladder** | Equal partners | One partner throws a kick; the other throws one back at the same level and then different levels. | 2 | 3 × 3 min | Timing, checking |
| KD8 | **Pad work with kick shield** | Holder / kicker | Holder calls a combination from Part 1 and holds mitts and a shield. Add a counter strike from the holder to train defense. | 2→3 | 5 × 3 min | Technique and power |
| KD9 | **Mirror footwork with kicks** | Leader / follower | Leader moves and throws a teep or a lead kick at 10% power; follower stays out of range or checks. | 2 | 3 × 2 min | Range |
| KD10 | **Switch-kick timing drill** | Feeder / kicker | Feeder moves forward with a jab; kicker steps off the line and throws a switch kick on the feeder's retreat. | 2 | 3 × 2 min | Switch-kick entry |
| KD11 | **Light clinch with one knee** | Equal partners | Collar tie, one knee to the body pad or hip, then release. | 2 | 3 × 2 min | Clinch rules |
| KD12 | **One-weapon sparring: legs only** | Equal partners | Only low kicks and checks. | 3 | 3 × 2 min | Low-kick offense and defense |
| KD13 | **Technical sparring** | Equal partners | Controlled, themed rounds (e.g. "teep and check"). | 3–4 | 3 × 2 min | Application |
| KD14 | **Combination relay** | Pair | One partner throws a combination; the other repeats it with one more step. | 2 | 3 × 3 min | Memory and flow |

### 2.1 Progressions
- Add a feint before the kick.
- Add a second kick on the same leg only after a reset.
- Add a counter-punch window: the holder throws one slow punch back.

### 2.2 Safety for kickboxing drills
- **Kicks to the legs hurt before they injure** — start at intensity 2, and never kick the knee joint (oblique drills target the thigh at intensity 2).
- Shin guards for all partner kicks.
- A failed check is the most common injury source; do not rush the progression.
- No kicks to the head except with a shield or at intensity 1.

## 3. Solo practice

| Drill | What to do | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Kick shadowboxing | Throw a combination with a full return to guard | 3 × 3 min |
| Heavy-bag combos | Throw K1–K12 on the bag, ten times each | 5 × 3 min |
| Balance drill | Hold the kick for two seconds before landing | 3 × 10 each side |
| Flexibility | Hip openers, hamstring and adductor stretches | 10 min |
